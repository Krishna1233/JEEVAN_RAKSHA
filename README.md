# JEEVAN_RAKSHA
Smart Flood Relief &amp; Victim Support System








## 🚀 Future Enhancements

The following features are planned for future versions of JEEVANRAKSHA 2.0:

1. **Donation Integration** – Secure online donation and payment facilities to support victims of different emergencies.
2. **Volunteer Management System** – Volunteer registration, verification and management.
3. **Unique Volunteer ID** – A unique identification number for every registered volunteer.
4. **Volunteer Database** – Secure storage and management of volunteer records.
5. **Relief Support Network** – Connecting verified donors, volunteers and people in need.
6. **Expanded Emergency Support** – Extending assistance beyond floods to accidents, fires, medical emergencies and other crises.

*These features are planned for future development and are not yet fully implemented.*
